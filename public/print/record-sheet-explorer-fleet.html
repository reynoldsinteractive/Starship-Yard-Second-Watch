Round sequence

  1. Power: split points
  2. Move: lowest Helm first
  3. Fire: highest Helm first
  4. Damage: shields, then hull; 3+ hull or a 12 = critical
  5. Damage control and Stress check

Core roll: 2d6 + roll bonus vs 6 / 8 / 10 / 12. Beat by 3+: success and +1 Momentum. Meet it: success. Miss by 1–2: success at a cost. Miss by more: it fails and the story moves on. Double six = +1 Momentum; double one = +1 Threat. Fleet profile. Second Watch rules: https://claude.ai/artifact/8EBgPUT6e1NfrZe2hK4jza

Quick reference

  1. Roll 2d6 + roll bonus (max +4) vs 6 Routine, 8 Standard, 10 Hard, 12 Heroic
  2. Advantage: roll 3d6, keep best 2 (a fitting facility)
  3. Disadvantage: keep worst 2 (missing or damaged system)
  4. Results: beat by 3+ success +1 Momentum · meet it success · miss by 1–2 success at a cost · miss by 3+ fail forward
  5. Momentum (table, max 6): spend 1 for +1, 2 for advantage or an extra action
  6. Invoke a ship trait for advantage; a compel gives the captain 1 Resolve
  7. Push a failed roll: 1 Stress (in a fight) or 1 Resolve
