Round sequence

Core roll: 2d6 + roll bonus vs 6 / 8 / 10 / 12. Advantage 3d6 keep best 2; disadvantage keep worst 2. Double six = critical, double one = complication. Bridge profile. Second Watch rules: https://claude.ai/artifact/8EBgPUT6e1NfrZe2hK4jza

Quick reference

  1. Roll 2d6 + roll bonus (max +4) vs 6 Routine, 8 Standard, 10 Hard, 12 Heroic
  2. Advantage: roll 3d6, keep best 2 (a fitting facility)
  3. Disadvantage: keep worst 2 (missing or damaged system)
  4. Double six: success plus a benefit. Double one: fail plus a complication
  5. Push a failed roll: 1 Stress (in a fight) or 1 Resolve
