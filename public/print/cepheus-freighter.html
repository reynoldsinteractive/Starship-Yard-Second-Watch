TL13 Long Haul HV-82410

Ship description

Using a 400-ton hull (8 Hull, 8 Structure), the Long Haul is a freighter built in Second Watch: Drydock. It mounts jump drive D, maneuver drive D, and power plant D, giving a performance of Jump-2 and 2-G acceleration. Fuel tankage of 96 tons supports the power plant for four weeks and one Jump-2. Adjacent to the bridge is a computer Model 5. The ship is equipped with Advanced sensors (DM+1). There are nine staterooms and no low berths. The ship has four hardpoints and one ton allocated for fire control. Installed on the hardpoints are a double turret (beam laser, beam laser). This ship has no screens. There are no small craft hangars. Cargo capacity is 173 tons. The hull is standard, and is armored with Crystaliron (4 points). Special features include escape pods for 9. The ship requires a crew of 9: three pilots, one navigator, two engineers, one medic, one gunner, one sensor operator. The ship carries no passengers. The ship costs MCr116.280 (including the standard design discount) and takes 60 weeks to build.
