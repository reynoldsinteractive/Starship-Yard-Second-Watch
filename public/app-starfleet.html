Every legend had a fleet behind it.

When a famous ship went missing, someone searched for it. When the big battle ended, someone pulled the survivors from the wreckage. You are those captains. Canon is the weather; the adventure is everything that happened next.

    Take it to any table

    The technical readout is a spec sheet with no game rules: pictures from three angles, dimensions, crew, power, weapons, traits and a deck summary. Any group can stat the ship for its own system from it.

    The Cepheus Engine export writes the ship up for the open 2D6 classic sci-fi rules that many space-trader games are built on: hull tons, drives, jump rating, turrets, crew and cargo, in the standard ship description format.

    Playing something else? Tell us which game and what numbers it needs, and we'll look at building the export.
