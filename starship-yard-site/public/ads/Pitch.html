The designerEvery choice in the shipyard shows up at the table. Skip the science labs and you will feel it on a survey mission. Overgun an escort and it hits hard but cracks under stress.
The TrekkieTurbolifts, transporter rooms and Jefferies tubes laid out deck by deck, era-correct hulls, and campaigns set in the gaps around events you already know, from the Xindi attack to the Burn.
The game nightTwo six-sided dice and one roll to learn. Each friend captains a ship in a task force, or the group shares one bridge. Away teams mix crews from every ship.
The AdmiralSet the power level, hand out orders, and borrow canon as the weather. The heroes stay offstage, so your table's story is never upstaged.
